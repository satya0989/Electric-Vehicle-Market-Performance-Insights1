# Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Performance & Insights

## 📌 Project Summary
This project analyzes the global Electric Vehicle (EV) market to uncover key trends in vehicle sales, pricing, battery technology (LFP, NMC, NCA, Sodium-Ion, Solid-State), and manufacturer performance. The insights directly address strategic questions for key industry stakeholders.

---

## 👥 Stakeholder Scenarios Addressed

### 1. Emily Johnson — EV Market Strategy Director
* **Objective:** Understand which vehicle categories contribute most to annual sales, identify leading manufacturers, and evaluate customer satisfaction across product segments.
* **Key Findings / Dashboard Insights:** 
  * SUVs and sedans lead total annual sales volume.
  * Premium manufacturers dominate customer satisfaction ratings due to extended driving range and faster charging speeds.

### 2. David Chen — Automotive Business Intelligence Manager
* **Objective:** Analyze manufacturer market share, battery technology distribution, geographic production trends, and competitive advantages.
* **Key Findings / Dashboard Insights:**
  * NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt) remains the most widely adopted battery technology, though LFP is rapidly gaining ground in budget-friendly segments.
  * Geographical analysis highlights Asia-Pacific and Europe as primary production and consumption hubs.

### 3. Dr. Sarah Williams — Sustainable Mobility Research Analyst
* **Objective:** Study how battery innovations and driving range improvements influence consumer adoption and support long-term environmental sustainability goals.
* **Key Findings / Dashboard Insights:**
  * A direct correlation exists between driving range improvements and higher consumer adoption rates across all vehicle categories.

## 📊 Technical Architecture & Workflow
1. **Data Collection & Extraction:** Sourced raw market data covering vehicle specifications and sales metrics.
2. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and processed data using Python (`pandas`) to handle null values and format metrics.
3. **Data Visualization & Dashboard:** Built an interactive executive dashboard using **Tableau Public**, incorporating filters for vehicle types, regions, and battery technologies.
4. **Testing & Integration:** Validated metrics against known industry benchmarks to ensure data accuracy.
